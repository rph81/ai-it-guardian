Security & vault

Connector passwords, tokens and keys are in an encrypted vault. It opens only when an admin or operator confirms with their passkey, and closes again when they sign out or after 30 minutes without activity.

Vault unlocked

How your secrets are protected

Your passkeyLives on your device (Face ID, Windows Hello, YubiKey). Never leaves it.
Your personal keyOpened only by your passkey. Stored encrypted; useless without it.
Vault keyA copy is sealed for each admin and operator. Never written to disk unencrypted.
Each secretEncrypted separately (AES-256-GCM) and bound to its connector.

Someone with full access to the server, including an AI tool, sees only encrypted data while the vault is locked.
