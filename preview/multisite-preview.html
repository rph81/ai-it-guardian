Guardian / Multi-site

Several customers, one console.

Every assessment, connector, schedule and ticket belongs to a site (a customer location). You switch sites at the top, or look at all of them at once. Each site can send tickets to your own PSA, to the customer's own system, or nowhere.

4 · What is per site, what stays global

Scope of each setting

Per site

  • Network range (scope) and exclusions
  • Connectors (AD, Microsoft 365, Azure, SSH/WinRM, NinjaOne…)
  • Schedule
  • Ticketing mode, mapping and thresholds
  • Runs, findings, Fix plan, attack paths, compliance, evidence
  • Fix (remediation) proposals and change log
  • Collector

Global (the MSP)

  • Users, passkeys, vault, security, audit log
  • AI key, model, agents
  • The MSP's own PSA connection (system + secret)
  • Notifications
  • Appearance
